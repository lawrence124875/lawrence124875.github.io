A tarot deck's 78 cards fall into two groups: the Major Arcana and the Minor Arcana. "Arcana" comes from Latin and means "secrets". Understanding the difference between them is the most important first step in learning tarot.

## The differences at a glance

| | Major Arcana | Minor Arcana |
|---|---|---|
| Cards | 22 | 56 |
| Numbering | 0–21 | Ace–10 plus 4 court cards in each suit |
| Themes | Big life lessons and turning points | Everyday events and moods |
| Time scale | Longer term, deeper | Shorter term, more concrete |
| In a spread | Usually carry more weight | Add detail and context |

## The Major Arcana: life's big themes

Each Major Arcana card has its own name and number, and shows a larger theme of life, such as beginnings, choices, challenges, endings and renewal. Many people see them as one complete journey of growth.

When **many Major Arcana cards** appear in one reading, it's often taken to mean the matter runs deep for you, or you're at an important turning point.

## The Minor Arcana: the details of daily life

Like a deck of playing cards, the Minor Arcana have four suits. Each suit is commonly linked to an element and an area of life:

| Suit | Common element | Common themes |
|---|---|---|
| Wands | Fire | Action, passion, creativity |
| Cups | Water | Emotions, relationships, intuition |
| Swords | Air | Thoughts, communication, conflict |
| Pentacles | Earth | Money, work, the body and material things |

Decks and traditions vary a little. These are the most common links.

### Number cards

Think of Ace to 10 as a process **from beginning to completion**. The Ace is a seed, the middle numbers are growth and change, and the 10 completes a stage.

### Court cards

The Page, Knight, Queen and King are often read as people or attitudes at different levels of maturity:

- **Page:** learning, curiosity, beginnings
- **Knight:** action, pursuit, drive
- **Queen:** inner mastery, nurturing, mature feeling
- **King:** outer mastery, control, mature action

## Can beginners use only the Major Arcana?

Yes. Many people start with just the 22 Major Arcana because there are fewer cards and the themes are vivid. Once you're comfortable, add the Minor Arcana for richer, more everyday readings.

## Summary

The Major Arcana tell you **what the lesson is**. The Minor Arcana tell you **how it shows up in daily life**. Together, they make a complete tarot deck.
