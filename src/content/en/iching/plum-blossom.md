Plum Blossom numerology (Meihua Yishu) is a way to cast an I Ching hexagram without coins or yarrow stalks. Legend says the Song-dynasty scholar Shao Yong saw two sparrows fighting on a plum branch and fall to the ground, and cast a hexagram from that very moment, which gave the method its name. Its core idea: **any number from the present moment can be a starting point for reflection.**

## Learn the trigram numbers first

The method maps numbers to the eight trigrams using the "Early Heaven" order:

| Number | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trigram | Qian | Dui | Li | Zhen | Xun | Kan | Gen | Kun |
| Image | Heaven | Lake | Fire | Thunder | Wind | Water | Mountain | Earth |

## Casting by date and time

The traditional method uses the Chinese lunar calendar and the twelve Earthly Branches, numbered 1 (Zi) to 12 (Hai), for the year and the two-hour period of the day:

1. **Upper trigram**: (year number + month + day) ÷ 8. Use the remainder; a remainder of 0 counts as 8.
2. **Lower trigram**: (year number + month + day + hour number) ÷ 8. Use the remainder.
3. **Changing line**: (year number + month + day + hour number) ÷ 6. Use the remainder; 0 counts as 6.

The two trigrams form the primary hexagram. Flip the changing line to get the changed hexagram.

### Example

Say the year number is 5, the lunar date is month 3, day 12, and the hour number is 7:

- Upper: 5 + 3 + 12 = 20; 20 ÷ 8 leaves 4 → **Zhen (Thunder)**
- Lower: 20 + 7 = 27; 27 ÷ 8 leaves 3 → **Li (Fire)**
- Changing line: 27 ÷ 6 leaves 3 → **third line**

## Casting with any two numbers

If you'd rather skip the calendar, think of (or ask the other person for) any two numbers:

1. First number ÷ 8, remainder → upper trigram
2. Second number ÷ 8, remainder → lower trigram
3. Sum of both ÷ 6, remainder → changing line

Some people also add the current hour so the result ties more closely to "right now".

## What makes Plum Blossom different

- **Always available:** no tools needed
- **Rooted in the moment:** the numbers come from now, so it focuses on your present state
- **Always one changing line:** every reading includes change, so you can focus on that one line

## Common questions

**Do I have to use the lunar calendar?** Traditionally yes, but some people use the everyday calendar. Pick one and stay consistent.

**What if the remainder is 0?** For trigrams, 0 counts as 8 (Kun). For the changing line, 0 counts as 6 (the top line).

**Is it better than the coin method?** Neither is better. Coins are great for beginners. Plum Blossom suits people who want to practise anywhere and get to know the trigrams.
